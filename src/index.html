The Aurora is a non-profit collective of highly-trained scholars independently creating and applying scientific knowledge through original research and methodology. We also make this available to professionals and organizations who would like to acquire or apply quantitative and human skills related to science, technology & data. We believe data and automation together with human activity can achieve the seemingly impossible and, nevertheless, that it is necessary to know the cases where one or the other is needed. Learn more about us, checkout our training and individual consulting, or talk to us directly about your ideas.
