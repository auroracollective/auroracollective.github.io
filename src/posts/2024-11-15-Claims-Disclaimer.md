This is an un[professional blog], do not confuse it with an [unprofessional] blog -- this is a formal medium, and bracket and parenthesis placement matters. It will therefore have posts that differ radically from companies that try to use a dry and confident tone to reassure and convey certainty -- which should irk anyone who has some understanding of statistics, as well as of science in general, as there is no such thing as absolute certainty. 

Our research, training, and advising/consulting is formal, mathematically and scientifically accurate. As a consequence, posts in this blog will often state hard mathematical and statistical facts, with all the propositions, lemmata, an theorems necessary. All statements we make are based on the best science available; nevertheless, we cannot confine ourselves in this safe space, because that is not where real-world problems live. To tackle these problems we must put ourselves out there and take the risks of surfing the bleeding edge. In that space, things get really complex, and actually intersting; there, we will still make strong statements, but we will also acknowledge the uncertainty around any prediction (statistical or otherwise).

These pages are therefore a space for exploration. When we negate the status of "professional blog", we dismiss the sanitized style and language that is required for the appearance of knowing it all, of always being in control (because if we can be certain of anything is that that is a lie). We forgo the cowardice of certainty and embrance the unknown. We are not sure, but we are very confident that the best way of navigating an unpredictable environment is to be honest about what we know, what we don't, why we get things right, why we got some wrong -- it's to be realistic, but also be brave.
