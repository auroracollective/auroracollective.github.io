The people behind the collective are front and center

Caetano Souto Maior
Caetano holds advanced degrees and experience across different domains of exact and natural sciences, like Supramolecular Chemistry and Computational Quantum Chemistry, Public Health and Epidemiology, a Ph.D. in Computational Biology, and several years of postdoctoral experience both in Systems Genomics of Sleep and Circadian Rhythms as well as in Hamiltonian Monte Carlo applied to Ordinary and Partial Differential Equation models. He has extensive teaching experience full-semester courses in advanced topics which include: Fundamental Calculus, Multivariable Calculus, Linear Algebra with Applications in Statistics and Machine Learning, Mathematical Modeling and Statistical Inference, Discrete Mathematics, in addition to several short workshops and seminars. He's a certified Software and Data Carpentries Instructor since 2020. He is also responsible for the hard task of writing everyone's description including one about himself in third person, so he needs to make sure his own does justice to his qualifications without overselling them;nevertheless, he will not shy away from nor spare any efforts in touting all the achievements of his Aurora Collective teammates.

Ana Ribeiro
Ana holds a Ph.D. in Immunology from the Gulbenkian Institute of Science with Postdoctoral training in Heme metabolism at the University of Maryland. Her unique trajectory combines this academic research experience with scientific attraction and professional development at internationally recognized institutions in Europe and the US, like the Vall d'Hebron Hospital research institutes and the National Institutes of Health (NIH). Ana has been using this blend to learn about what is important for professionals to grow and to guide them through the many possible routes that lead to their individual and organizational goals. Ana is commited to developing strategies that promote inclusive, equitable and transparent scientific research practices, as well as to transform institutional culture into something that equips professionals with what they need to succeed and that represents the diversity of communities and organizations, one that creates a sense of belonging and helps them to thrive.

Daniel Damineli
Daniel has advanced degrees in plant biology, a Ph.D. in computational biology from the Gulbenkian Institute and extensive postdoctoral experience in world-reknown institutions including the University of São Paulo, the Gulbenkian Institute, and the University of Maryland. In addition to academic accolades and metrics like citations, undergraduates taught, publication indices, positions as journal reviewer, usually counted in the hundreds to thousands, Daniel is someone who is able to do research in topics as far apart as binary star systems, polen tubes, advanced data visualization and presentation and CHUKNORRIS. Daniel can do it all: formulate hypotheses, develop mathematical models, analyze data, present beautiful visuals that make the covers of top journals, ride a monocycle, and play the trumpet, but not always at the same time.
